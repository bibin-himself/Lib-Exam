>SANJAY and TRISHNA (often spelled Thrishna):
 - Library automation software packages developed in India during the 1990s.
 - They were built by augmenting UNESCO's CDS/ISIS (V2.3) 
 - Meets the local script and functional housekeeping demands of Indian libraries.
 - Both Catalyzed and sponsored by NISSAT (National Information System for Science and Technology)

 >SANJAY
  Developed by a team of software professionals at DESIDOC.
  SANJAY modified CDS/ISIS into a complete Integrated Library Management Software (ILMS) package


 >THRISHNA 
  Developed by NISSAT in close collaboration with NISTADS 
   Break language barriers in Indian library cataloguing by adding native multilingual support to CDS/ISIS

>>>>> INIS <<<<<  -----> Module 6 line 606
 INIS (1970)
  IAEA-sponsored international nuclear information system headquartered in Vienna.

  Decentralized, computer-based, mission-oriented network  Provides Atomindex, Document Delivery Service, CAS, SDI, Retrospective Search, Online Retrieval, and INIS Thesaurus-based indexing

  India's participation coordinated through BARC, Mumbai.






>>>>> MEDLARS <<<<<  -----> Module 6 line 658
  Medical Literature Analysis and Retrieval System
  1964
  By the National Library of Medicine (USA)
  World's first large-scale computerized biomedical information retrieval system.

  It used MeSH for subject indexing,
  Produced Index Medicus, offered CAS, SDI, literature searches, and bibliographic services,

  Evolved into MEDLINE (1971) and PubMed (1997).
